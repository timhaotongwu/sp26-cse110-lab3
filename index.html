New Business

The team discussed a possible Halloween community event for students. The main goal of the event is to create a fun, welcoming, and inclusive environment where students can relax, meet new people, and participate in seasonal activities.

Several theme ideas were proposed, including a haunted house, a costume party, and a horror movie night. The team also discussed activity options such as pumpkin carving, trick-or-treat booths, and a scary maze.

One important point was that the event should not only be entertaining, but also realistic to organize. This means the team needs to think carefully about budget, location, timing, decorations, snacks, and prizes.

Activities

  • Trick-or-Treat Booths

Logistics

  1. Location: UCSD campus or indoor hall
  2. Budget: decorations, snacks, and prizes
  3. Time: evening, around 7:00 PM to 10:00 PM

Roles

  • Organizer: plan event structure
  • Designer: create posters and decorations
  • Tech: manage music and lighting
  • Marketing: promote the event on social media
